# Frontend overview

What the Angular app contains today and the details a change must keep. The rules are in `.claude/rules/frontend.md` and `testing.md`; the HTTP shapes in `docs/api-contract.md`.

## Shared UI (`shared/ui/`)

- `form-field/`: `<app-form-field label [hint] [messages]>` around one control marked `appFormControl` (needs `id` and `ngModel`); renders `<label for>`, the hint and the message for the first error key, sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` once touched or submitted; `form[appFocusFirstInvalid]` focuses the first invalid control on submit. Control look is the global `.form-control` in `src/styles/_form-controls.scss`; components only space fields.
- `password-reveal/`: eye button "Zobrazit heslo" (`aria-pressed`, `aria-controls`) switching the input between `password` and `text`; every password field uses it.
- `live-notice/`: `<app-live-notice [notice]>` takes a `Notice` (`{ kind: 'status' | 'alert', text }` or `null`) and keeps a `role="status"` and a `role="alert"` region rendered, showing the text as `.ui-notice--status` or `--alert` in the matching one, so a screen reader announces it; every admin page's action line uses it.
- `copy-button/`: `<app-copy-button [value] what="číslo účtu">`: "Kopírovat" plus the hidden `what`; says "Zkopírováno" for 2 s and in a `role="status"` line; a visible sentence when the browser refuses.
- `qr-code/`: `<app-qr-code [matrix] [variant] label>` draws a QR code from the backend's module matrix (`qr-matrix.ts`: `QrMatrix`, `isDark`, `alignmentCentres`) as inline SVG with a 4-module quiet zone, `role="img"` named by `label`. `branded` (default, the payment look chosen by Jakub on 2026-10-23): round dots in `--color-qr-module`, rounded finder squares and alignment marks in `--color-qr-mark`, and over the centre a blue disc with the logo's star (`--color-logo`, `--color-logo-fill`; the matrix must be level H); when the matrix changes it morphs in a wave from the star outwards (kept dots pulse, gone ones shrink, new ones grow in the mark colour and settle to the module colour, ~2.6 s, Web Animations API, nothing under `prefers-reduced-motion`). `plain`: black squares, used for the authenticator link. `pngDataUrl(sidePx)` paints a copy with the token colours and returns a PNG made on a canvas.
- `side-nav/`: `<app-side-nav [groups] label menuLabel [navId]>` renders an area's two-column layout: a sticky panel left (`--side-nav-width` 240 px, under the top bar via `--top-bar-height`, the whole layout at most `--side-nav-layout-max` 1320 px) with a `nav` (`aria-label` = `label`) of `SideNavGroup`s (`{ id, label | null, items: SideNavItem[] }`; a titled group's `ul` is `aria-labelledby` its title, an untitled one has no label) of `SideNavItem` links (`{ path, label, exact? }`, `routerLinkActive` with `aria-current="page"`, prefix match unless `exact`), and the projected content (`<ng-content />`, a `router-outlet`) right. Up to 899 px the panel hides behind the pill button `menuLabel` (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` = `navId`), opens below it as a card and closes on every `NavigationEnd` and on Escape inside it (focus back to the button).
- `radio-group/`: `[appRadioGroup]` on a group of `role="radio"` buttons makes it a `radiogroup` that answers the arrow keys, Home and End by focusing and clicking the next choice; the buttons bind `[tabindex]` 0 for the chosen one and -1 for the rest (sign-in dialog: donor type and sign-in method; password reset: method).
- `markdown/`: `parseMarkdown(text)` (`markdown-parser.ts`) turns a news or project body into blocks (paragraph with line breaks, heading level 2 or 3, list, quote) of inline nodes (text, bold, italic, link, break), the subset in `docs/api-contract.md` ("`content` — public CMS"); a link keeps only an `http(s)`, `mailto:` or site-relative address, everything else, HTML included, stays text. `<app-markdown [text]>` renders the tree with its own elements through a recursive `ng-template` and `{{ }}` bindings (no `innerHTML`), in the prose look (70 ch, serif headings). `<app-markdown-toolbar [target]>` above a textarea: the `role="group"` "Formátování textu" with Tučně, Kurzíva, Nadpis, Odrážky, Číslování and Odkaz, each wrapping the selection or prefixing its lines (a placeholder word selected when nothing was), then focusing the textarea and dispatching `input` so `ngModel` follows. `<app-markdown-preview [text] previewId>`: the warning "Text můžete formátovat tlačítky nad polem … Před zveřejněním si zkontrolujte v náhledu, jak bude na webu vypadat. …" (`ui-callout--warning`), the toggle "Ukázat náhled" / "Skrýt náhled" (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) and the region "Náhled textu" ("Zatím není co ukázat." when empty). Texts under `ui.markdown.*`; specs beside each.
- `dialog/`: `<app-dialog labelledBy [wide] (dismissed)>` on a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` (focus kept inside, page inert, focus to the `autofocus` control; Esc, × and backdrop emit `dismissed`; focus returns to the opener on destroy; full-screen up to 480 px).

## Layout

`site-header/`: sticky `nav` named "Hlavní menu", desktop links and a burger menu (signal state; `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="mobile-menu"`, "Otevřít/Zavřít menu"; Esc closes and refocuses the button; outside click, a link or the focus leaving it closes; after a sign-in from its "Přihlásit se" the focus moves to the `nav` itself (`tabindex="-1"`, no outline), so no control stays highlighted and the next Tab starts at the logo). `site-footer/`: columns and bottom bar with the privacy link. `public-shell/` asks `SetupRedirect.checkOnce()` (`features/operations/setup/setup-redirect.ts`) once per page load: while `GET /api/operations/setup` says `required`, it navigates to `/prvni-nastaveni`; a failure is ignored, so a running site never depends on it. Both shells start with the skip link "Přeskočit na obsah" (global `.skip-link`, fixed top-left, shown on keyboard focus) to `main#obsah`: in `public-shell/` the `main` around the pages, in `donor-shell/` (`/darce`, `/sprava`) the content column of `side-nav`, after the top bar `header` and the panel `nav`. `public-shell/` also follows navigation for keyboard and screen-reader users: after a route change (not the first load, not back/forward, not while a dialog is open) it focuses the page `h1` unless the page already moved the focus into `main`, and after a link to a section (`/#kontakt`, also when clicked again) it focuses that section; both get `tabindex="-1"` and no ring.

## donations (services and helpers)

- `payment-qr-api.ts`: `paymentQr(amount, variableSymbol, purpose = 'DONATION')` → SPAYD, `qrCode` (module matrix), account; sends `purpose` only for `ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION`.
- `donations-api.ts`: `GET /api/donations/me` and `/me/summary` (the API's `year` filter has no caller); `requestBankCheck()` (POST) and `bankCheck()` (GET) on `/me/bank-check`; `checkBankUntilDone()` posts and polls every 2 s while `checking`, at most 45 times, emitting every answer; `singleReceipt(id)` and `yearlyReceipt(year)` fetch the PDFs as blobs and return `DownloadedFile` (`blob`, `fileName` from `Content-Disposition`: `filename*=UTF-8''` first, then `filename=`, else `potvrzeni-o-daru.pdf` / `potvrzeni-o-darech-<year>.pdf`).
- `bank-accounts-api.ts`: `GET /api/donations/me/bank-accounts` (`ACTIVE`, `AWAITING_PAYMENT`, `DISCONNECTED`, `shared`, `pairingExcluded`, `verificationPaymentOn`, `symbolOffer` with `variableSymbol`, `paymentCount`, `firstMonth`/`lastMonth` as `YYYY-MM`, and `confirmedSymbols`), `POST …/{id}/disconnect|reconnect` (204), `confirmSymbol` / `declineSymbol` on `POST …/{id}/symbol-claim/confirm` (`{ pairedCount, changedYearlyReceipts }`) and `…/decline` (204) with `{ variableSymbol }`; `DonationItem.pairedBy` (`VARIABLE_SYMBOL` | `SENDER_ACCOUNT` | `CONFIRMED_SYMBOL` | null), `senderAccount`, `confirmedSymbol` and `receiptAvailable`.
- `donation-month.ts`: `formatMonth('2025-01')` → `leden 2025` (names `@@donor.month.january` … `december`), `formatMonthRange(first, last)` → `říjen 2026`, `březen–říjen 2026` or `leden 2025 – říjen 2026`, with no-break spaces and no locale data.
- `file-download.ts` `saveBlob(file)`: object URL on a hidden `<a download>` clicked inside the page, revoked in `setTimeout(…, 0)` (Safari and Firefox cancel a synchronously revoked download). `donation-date.ts`: `YYYY-MM-DD` → `15. 03. 2026` without locale data; `formatBankTime` → Prague `18. 10. 2026 10:05`. `donation-amount.ts`: whole crowns without decimals, hellers always two (`1 000,50`). `testing/` fixtures.

## operations

## Theme, fonts, assets

- The theme is a folder chosen at build time: `src/themes/nova-nadeje/_theme.scss` declares the `@font-face` rules and every token on `:root` (inventory in `frontend.md`), `styles.scss` loads it as `@use 'theme'` through `stylePreprocessorOptions.includePaths` (`src/styles` and the theme folder), and the configuration `control-theme` swaps in `src/themes/control/` (a deliberately different look with system fonts, built and checked by `e2e/theme.spec.ts` in CI only; `scripts/check-themes.mjs`, part of `npm run lint:styles`, keeps the token sets equal). Fonts: Playfair Display and Inter as self-hosted variable WOFF2 (latin and latin-ext from Fontsource, OFL licences beside them in `src/themes/nova-nadeje/fonts/`); the build hashes them into `/media/`, which nginx caches as immutable. Type sizes, weights, line heights, letter spacing, the spacing scale (`--space-unit` × n) and transition durations are tokens there too; `src/styles/_breakpoints.scss` holds the named widths and the `bp.down`/`bp.up` mixins (on the include path, so components write `@use 'breakpoints' as bp;`). `src/styles/ui/` holds the shared building blocks (global `ui-` classes for page head, card, notice and load state, badge, button, table, form errors and segmented choices, the standalone panel pages, and the public site's section width, eyebrow, page intro band and calls to action, list in `frontend.md`), used by every page of the administration, the standalone pages (password reset, e-mail verification, connected application, first setup), the sign-in dialog, the cards of `/darce` and the public pages (home sections, news and project detail, privacy policy, not found, footer); their SCSS is linted with the components. `npm run lint:styles` fails on a raw colour, `font-family`, font size, weight, line height, letter spacing, `border-radius`, shadow, `px` spacing (except 1 px), transition duration or `width` media query outside the theme.
- `public/images/`: hero in 1024/1536 px with `srcset` (the source photo is 1536 px wide, so wider screens scale it up), `sizes="max(100vw, 150vh, 900px)"` and `fetchpriority="high"`; about and donate in 720/1440 px; project and news cards at 600 px; every photo below the hero has `loading="lazy"` written before `src`/`srcset` (Angular sets attributes in template order) plus `width`/`height`, so photos hidden on phones are never downloaded there. Convert a new photo with Pillow or `cwebp` at quality about 78; no file is kept that no template names.
- Icons: Jakub's app icon, the logo's smiling star in a blue gradient circle with a white rim (viewBox 1036 wide). `favicon.svg` is that icon; its `<style>` hides the face (`g.face`) at 24 px or less (Chromium measures CSS px, so a 16 px image has no face on a 2x screen either; `e2e/home-assets.spec.ts`). `favicon.ico` holds 16, 32 and 48 px renders of it; `app-icon.png` is its 512 px render with transparent corners (the ZimaOS icon URL); `apple-touch-icon.png` is 180 px on opaque RGB, the gradient filling the whole square without the circle so iOS rounds the corners itself. The cloud ImageMagick has no SVG renderer: render with Playwright's Chromium (`omitBackground`) and pack with `convert 16.png 32.png 48.png favicon.ico`.
