# Frontend overview

What the Angular app contains today and the details a change must keep. The rules are in `.claude/rules/frontend.md`, `testing.md` and `testing-frontend.md`; the HTTP shapes in `docs/api-contract.md`.

- [`frontend/shell-routing-auth.md`](frontend/shell-routing-auth.md): `index.html`, zoom, touch, the iPhone status bar, the `core/` services (reload, navigation, connectivity, device, version, live, updates, push, errors, diagnostics, drafts, auth), the routes and the bounds on client work.
- [`frontend/shared-ui.md`](frontend/shared-ui.md): the shared components and directives of `shared/ui/`.
- [`frontend/layout.md`](frontend/layout.md): the scrollbar gutter, the chunk-load notice, the offline status, the app shell, switching tabs and list rows.
- [`frontend/content.md`](frontend/content.md): the public pages of the tabs, the article pages, the news and projects pages, the Přispět band and the privacy policy.
- [`frontend/contact.md`](frontend/contact.md): the contact form.
- [`frontend/donations.md`](frontend/donations.md): payment details, banks, SPAYD, the payment panel, a gift's arrival, the receipt PDF and the verification page.
- [`frontend/operations.md`](frontend/operations.md): the first setup.
- [`frontend/donors.md`](frontend/donors.md): sign-in, e-mail verification, connecting an app, password reset, the donor area and the kept answers.
- [`frontend/admin.md`](frontend/admin.md): the administration at `/sprava`, page by page.
- [`frontend/theme-fonts-assets.md`](frontend/theme-fonts-assets.md): the theme, light and dark appearance, surfaces, images, icons, the launch screen and the web app manifest.
- [`frontend/motion.md`](frontend/motion.md): the motion system: its rules, timing, tokens, vocabulary, reduced motion, page and title transitions, testing in WebKit and the perceptible thresholds.
- [`frontend/tests.md`](frontend/tests.md): the specs and the test setup.
- [`frontend/conventions.md`](frontend/conventions.md): the full text of the frontend rules: Angular 22, i18n, UI, UX and testing.
- [`frontend/not-implemented.md`](frontend/not-implemented.md): what the frontend does not do yet.
