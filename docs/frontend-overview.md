# Frontend overview

What the Angular app contains today and the details a change must keep. The rules are in `.claude/rules/frontend.md` and `testing.md`; the HTTP shapes in `docs/api-contract.md`.

## Shell, routing, auth

- `src/index.html`: title, meta description, Open Graph tags (`og:image` and `og:url` wait for the real domain), icons (`favicon.ico` with `sizes="32x32"` so Chrome takes `favicon.svg`; `apple-touch-icon.png`); loads nothing from other sites. `main.ts` bootstraps and references `@angular/localize`. `styles.scss` loads the theme, reset, scrollbar, keyframes.
- `app.ts` renders `<router-outlet />` and the sign-in dialog while `LoginDialogService.isOpen()` (re-created on every open so fields and panels start clean; its "Přihlásit se / Registrace" switch carries `aria-pressed`). `app.config.ts`: `provideRouter` with `withInMemoryScrolling` (anchor scrolling for `routerLink="/" fragment="about"` links), `provideHttpClient(withFetch(), withInterceptors([authInterceptor]))`, session restore on start.
- `app.routes.ts`: `''` → `layout/public-shell` (header, footer) with children `'obnova-hesla'` → `features/donors/password-reset/` (title "Nové heslo | Nová naděje – nadační fond") and `''` → `features/content/content.routes.ts` (`''` home, `'zasady-ochrany-osobnich-udaju'` privacy policy, `'**'` not-found; a route cannot have both `children` and `loadChildren`, so the content routes hang under a child of their own); `'donor'` → `layout/donor-shell` with `features/donors/donors.routes.ts` (`'prehled'` dashboard) behind `core/auth/donor-guard.ts`. Unknown addresses keep their URL and show `not-found`; no top-level redirect.
- `core/auth/`: `donor.ts` (DTOs `Donor`, `RegisterDonorRequest`, `AuthResponse`, `PASSWORD_MIN_LENGTH` 12 shared by registration and reset), `auth-api.ts` (`/api/donors/auth/{register,login,refresh,logout}`, `password-reset/{request,confirm}`, `/api/donors/me`), `auth-store.ts` (signals `donor`, `status`, `signedIn`; access token in memory; one refresh shared between callers; `restore()` on start calls `refresh` only when the `localStorage` flag `nfnn.donor-session` says a session may exist), `auth-interceptor.ts` (Bearer on `/api/**` except the auth endpoints; parses an `application/problem+json` Blob body into the `HttpErrorResponse` first; on 401 `auth/expired-token` refreshes once and retries), `donor-guard.ts` (waits for the restore, else goes home and opens the sign-in dialog), `login-dialog.service.ts` (`open(mode = 'login')`, `LoginDialogMode` `login` | `register` | `reset`, read once by the dialog as its initial tab), `api-problem.ts` (`describeProblem(failure, generic)`: title, detail and field messages in one line, or `generic`), `testing/` fixtures.

## Shared UI (`shared/ui/`)

- `form-field/`: `<app-form-field label [hint] [messages]>` around one control marked `appFormControl` (needs `id` and `ngModel`); renders `<label for>`, the hint and the message for the first error key, sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` once touched or submitted; `form[appFocusFirstInvalid]` focuses the first invalid control on submit. Control look is the global `.form-control` in `src/styles/_form-controls.scss`; components only space fields.
- `password-reveal/`: eye button "Zobrazit heslo" (`aria-pressed`, `aria-controls`) switching the input between `password` and `text`; every password field uses it.
- `copy-button/`: `<app-copy-button [value] what="číslo účtu">`: "Kopírovat" plus the hidden `what`; says "Zkopírováno" for 2 s and in a `role="status"` line; a visible sentence when the browser refuses.
- `dialog/`: `<app-dialog labelledBy [wide] (dismissed)>` on a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` (focus kept inside, page inert, focus to the `autofocus` control; Esc, × and backdrop emit `dismissed`; focus returns to the opener on destroy; full-screen up to 480 px).

## Layout

`site-header/`: sticky nav, desktop links and a burger menu (signal state; `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="mobile-menu"`, "Otevřít/Zavřít menu"; Esc closes and refocuses the button; outside click or a link closes). `site-footer/`: columns and bottom bar with the privacy link.

## content

- `home/` composes `sections/`: `hero` (full-bleed photo, copy, CTA), `stats-bar` (years counted from the registry entry of 28 October 2005 on the visitor's day), `about`, `projects` (plain cards, nothing to click), `news` (placeholder content, no links until news pages exist), `donate-cta`: preset or custom amount (whole crowns with spaces, hint after leaving the field), QR from `PaymentQrApi` (debounced, latest request wins) with "Uložit QR kód", bank details (account, amount, the signed-in donor's variable symbol, IBAN), each with a copy button; a note says the gift shows in the overview once booked or, without a symbol, to sign in first ("Založit účet", "Přihlásit se" open the dialog); on phones the bank details precede the QR (CSS `order`).
- `privacy-policy/` ("Zásady ochrany osobních údajů"), `not-found/` ("Tuhle stránku jsme nenašli", links home and to the contact form, `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` while shown because nginx answers 200 for every path).

## contact

`contact-api.ts` (`POST /api/contact/messages`); `contact-form/` with honeypot, 429 and generic errors, and a line under the button that the data serves only to handle the message, linking the policy (legitimate interest, no consent wording).

## donations (services and helpers)

- `payment-qr-api.ts`: `paymentQr(amount, variableSymbol, purpose = 'DONATION')` → SPAYD, PNG, account; sends `purpose` only for `ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION`.
- `donations-api.ts`: `GET /api/donations/me` and `/me/summary` (the API's `year` filter has no caller); `requestBankCheck()` (POST) and `bankCheck()` (GET) on `/me/bank-check`; `checkBankUntilDone()` posts and polls every 2 s while `checking`, at most 45 times, emitting every answer; `singleReceipt(id)` and `yearlyReceipt(year)` fetch the PDFs as blobs and return `DownloadedFile` (`blob`, `fileName` from `Content-Disposition`: `filename*=UTF-8''` first, then `filename=`, else `potvrzeni-o-daru.pdf` / `potvrzeni-o-darech-<year>.pdf`).
- `bank-accounts-api.ts`: `GET /api/donations/me/bank-accounts` (`ACTIVE`, `AWAITING_PAYMENT`, `DISCONNECTED`, `shared`, `pairingExcluded`), `POST …/{id}/disconnect|reconnect` (204); `DonationItem.pairedBy` (`VARIABLE_SYMBOL` | `SENDER_ACCOUNT` | null) and `senderAccount`.
- `file-download.ts` `saveBlob(file)`: object URL on a hidden `<a download>` clicked inside the page, revoked in `setTimeout(…, 0)` (Safari and Firefox cancel a synchronously revoked download). `donation-date.ts`: `YYYY-MM-DD` → `15. 03. 2026` without locale data; `formatBankTime` → Prague `18. 10. 2026 10:05`. `donation-amount.ts`: whole crowns without decimals, hellers always two (`1 000,50`). `testing/` fixtures.

## donors

- `login-dialog/`: sign-in and registration through `AuthStore`, the problem's title and detail on failure, "Příliš mnoho pokusů z této adresy. Zkuste to prosím za chvíli znovu." on 429; the "Obnovit heslo" tab posts the trimmed address through `AuthApi.requestPasswordReset` ("E-mail odeslán!" on 202, its own sentence on 429, `describeProblem` otherwise). `donor-field-messages.ts` holds the name, street, city and postal code messages shared with the profile card.
- `password-reset/` (`/obnova-hesla#token=…`): reads the token once and removes it with `Location.replaceState`; asks for the password twice ("Hesla se neshodují." without a request); posts `AuthApi.confirmPasswordReset`; on 204 calls `AuthStore.logout()` if a session is held and offers the sign-in dialog; `donors/invalid-password-reset-token` and a missing token offer the dialog's reset tab (`LoginDialogService.open('reset')`); other failures show `describeProblem` in the form's alert line; each panel's `<h1 id="password-reset-title" tabindex="-1">` takes focus after a switch.
- `dashboard/`: `top-bar/` (sticky header: logo and "← Hlavní stránka", "Domů" up to 420 px, both to `/` without ending the session; the donor's name with a person icon, hidden up to 768 px, no greeting because names are not declined; red "Odhlásit se" in `--color-danger*`). `symbol-card/` (variable symbol with a copy button, "Přispět znovu" to `/#donate`, where the home page puts the symbol into the QR). `profile-card/` ("Údaje na potvrzení o daru": name, IČO for a company, address, e-mail, receipt language; "Upravit údaje" turns it into a form sending name, address and `preferredLocale` through `AuthStore.updateProfile` (`PATCH /api/donors/me`); e-mail and IČO read-only; focus to the first field on edit and back to the button after save or cancel; the hint says a new language reaches only receipts downloaded for the first time; a `role="status"` line confirms the save; `sessionEnded` is answered like a failed load).
- `bank-accounts-card/` ("Moje bankovní účty", below the payments): a round 32 px "?" button "Jak přiřazujeme platby" (`aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`) expands a `role="region"` panel, collapsed by default, explaining the pairing rules (symbol, verified account, accounts that pair only with the symbol, other symbol and symbol 0, switching pairing off and on, shared account, the e-mail) in true-to-backend Czech with the donor's symbol; Esc collapses and refocuses; the text is capped at 520 px. One row per account with status label, hint, "ověřeno …", "vypnuto od …" (the day after `disconnectedOn`) and the gifts without a symbol. A shared account's hint is an amber warning box (`account-hint--warning`, `--color-warning*`, hidden "Upozornění:") that never names the other donor, in the list and in the add dialog alike. A `pairingExcluded` account keeps its chip ("Ověřený" / "Párování vypnuto"); its live row says only payments with the symbol pair now; an active one offers no "Vypnout párování", an `AWAITING_PAYMENT` one reads "Jen s variabilním symbolem" and keeps "Vypnout párování", a disconnected one offers no "Zapnout párování" and says why on its newest row. "Vypnout párování" asks an inline confirmation (focus on "Nechat zapnuté"); "Zapnout párování" shows only on the newest row of its account number; both reload the list, announce in `role="status"` and focus the card heading; problems go through `describeProblem` in `role="alert"`, 404/409 reload. "Přidat účet" opens an `app-dialog` with the 1 Kč instructions, the QR from `paymentQr(1, symbol, 'ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION')` (message "Ověření účtu Nadační fond Nová naděje"), account, message and symbol; it watches in a `role="status"` box ("Čekáme na vaši platbu…" with a spinner): 30 s after opening and 30 s after each check ended (the backend's global pause) it runs `checkBankUntilDone()` and reloads the list, emitting `bankChecked`; an account `ACTIVE` now that was not when the dialog opened turns the box into a drawn tick "Účet … je ověřený" and ends the watch; a hidden tab pauses and checks at once when visible; after 20 checks "Platba zatím nedorazila" with "Kontrolovat dál"; animations stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`. A payment paired by `SENDER_ACCOUNT` carries the tag "bez VS · …3333/2010" in its purpose cell (full sentence as `title` and hidden text).
- Dashboard body: stat cards, year toggle and payments table from `DonationsApi` (summary and list on open; `state` loading/ready/error, retry, empty state; a 401 `auth/unauthenticated` or a session lost by a failed refresh sends the donor home with the sign-in dialog). Receipts: "Stáhnout PDF" (not on a `REFUNDED` row) and "↓ PDF za rok" through `saveBlob`; the yearly button is disabled with "Potvrzení za rok … bude ke stažení po skončení roku." above the summary's `yearlyReceiptsAvailableThrough` (no calendar in the frontend); a running download disables its button ("Připravujeme PDF…"); failures show in a `role="alert"` line in the card the download started from (the problem title for `donations/year-not-yet-completed` and `donations/yearly-receipt-not-available`, the title of a 404 on a single receipt after which summary and list are fetched again, else "Potvrzení se nepodařilo stáhnout. Zkuste to prosím znovu."). Bank check: on open and on "Obnovit" it posts `requestBankCheck`, shows "Kontrolujeme nové platby v bance…" (button disabled) while `checking`, polls every 2 s at most 45 times, then shows "Údaje z banky k …" and refetches when `bankDataAsOf` changed (always after "Obnovit"); a failed check leaves only the button. A note under the payments explains the hourly search, the 30 s limit, bank delays and the symbol. Every payment cell carries `data-label`, so up to 640 px each row is a card; amounts right-aligned with `--numeric-figures`.

## Theme, fonts, assets

- `src/styles/themes/_nova-nadeje.scss` declares the `@font-face` rules and every token on `:root` (inventory in `frontend.md`). Fonts: Playfair Display and Inter as self-hosted variable WOFF2 (latin and latin-ext from Fontsource, OFL licences beside them in `src/styles/themes/fonts/`); the build hashes them into `/media/`, which nginx caches as immutable. `npm run lint:styles` fails on a raw colour, `font-family`, `border-radius` or shadow outside the theme.
- `public/images/`: hero in 1280/1920/2560 px with `srcset`, `sizes="max(100vw, 150vh, 900px)"` and `fetchpriority="high"`; about and donate in 720/1440 px; project and news cards at 600 px; every photo below the hero has `loading="lazy"` written before `src`/`srcset` (Angular sets attributes in template order) plus `width`/`height`, so photos hidden on phones are never downloaded there. Convert a new photo with Pillow or `cwebp` at quality about 78; no file is kept that no template names.
- Icons: `favicon.svg` is the logo's star in a square viewBox 840 units wide, strokes 64 (face 52), white fill; its `<style>` hides the face (`g.face`) at 24 px or less (Chromium measures CSS px, so a 16 px image has no face on a 2x screen either; `e2e/home-assets.spec.ts`). `favicon.ico` holds 16, 32 and 48 px renders; `apple-touch-icon.png` is 180 px, viewBox 1060 wide (wider margin for iOS rounding), stroke 40 (face 32) on opaque white. The cloud ImageMagick has no SVG renderer: render with Playwright's Chromium (`omitBackground`) and pack with `convert 16.png 32.png 48.png favicon.ico`.
