# Frontend overview

What the Angular app contains today and the details a change must keep. The rules are in `.claude/rules/frontend.md` and `testing.md`; the HTTP shapes in `docs/api-contract.md`.

## Shell, routing, auth

- `src/index.html`: title, meta description, `<link rel="canonical">` and Open Graph tags (`og:url`, `og:image` `https://nfnn.cz/images/og-image.jpg` 1200×630 JPEG, a placeholder cut from the hero until real content arrives, `twitter:card`); `core/seo/canonical-link.ts` (`CanonicalLink.start()` from an app initializer, `SITE_ORIGIN` `https://nfnn.cz`) rewrites the canonical link and `og:url` to the path of every navigation without query or fragment; `public/robots.txt` keeps crawlers out of `/api/`, `/mcp`, `/darce/`, `/sprava/` and the link pages and names `public/sitemap.xml` (home and the privacy policy; news and project pages wait for a generated sitemap); icons (`favicon.ico` with `sizes="32x32"` so Chrome takes `favicon.svg`; `apple-touch-icon.png`); loads nothing from other sites. `main.ts` bootstraps and references `@angular/localize`. `styles.scss` loads the theme, reset, scrollbar, keyframes.
- `app.ts` renders `<router-outlet />` and the sign-in dialog while `LoginDialogService.isOpen()` (re-created on every open so fields and panels start clean; its "Přihlásit se / Registrace" switch carries `aria-pressed`). `app.config.ts`: `provideRouter` with `withInMemoryScrolling` (anchor scrolling for `routerLink="/" fragment="o-nas"` links; the home sections carry Czech ids without diacritics: `uvod`, `o-nas`, `projekty`, `aktuality`, `prispet`, `kontakt`), `provideHttpClient(withFetch(), withInterceptors([authInterceptor]))`, session restore on start.

## Shared UI (`shared/ui/`)

- `form-field/`: `<app-form-field label [hint] [messages]>` around one control marked `appFormControl` (needs `id` and `ngModel`); renders `<label for>`, the hint and the message for the first error key, sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` once touched or submitted; `form[appFocusFirstInvalid]` focuses the first invalid control on submit. Control look is the global `.form-control` in `src/styles/_form-controls.scss`; components only space fields.
- `password-reveal/`: eye button "Zobrazit heslo" (`aria-pressed`, `aria-controls`) switching the input between `password` and `text`; every password field uses it.
- `copy-button/`: `<app-copy-button [value] what="číslo účtu">`: "Kopírovat" plus the hidden `what`; says "Zkopírováno" for 2 s and in a `role="status"` line; a visible sentence when the browser refuses.
- `qr-code/`: `<app-qr-code [matrix] [variant] label>` draws a QR code from the backend's module matrix (`qr-matrix.ts`: `QrMatrix`, `isDark`, `alignmentCentres`) as inline SVG with a 4-module quiet zone, `role="img"` named by `label`. `branded` (default, the payment look chosen by Jakub on 2026-10-23): round dots in `--color-qr-module`, rounded finder squares and alignment marks in `--color-qr-mark`, and over the centre a blue disc with the logo's star (`--color-logo`, `--color-logo-fill`; the matrix must be level H); when the matrix changes it morphs in a wave from the star outwards (kept dots pulse, gone ones shrink, new ones grow in the mark colour and settle to the module colour, ~2.6 s, Web Animations API, nothing under `prefers-reduced-motion`). `plain`: black squares, used for the authenticator link. `pngDataUrl(sidePx)` paints a copy with the token colours and returns a PNG made on a canvas.
- `dialog/`: `<app-dialog labelledBy [wide] (dismissed)>` on a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` (focus kept inside, page inert, focus to the `autofocus` control; Esc, × and backdrop emit `dismissed`; focus returns to the opener on destroy; full-screen up to 480 px).

## Layout

`site-header/`: sticky nav, desktop links and a burger menu (signal state; `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="mobile-menu"`, "Otevřít/Zavřít menu"; Esc closes and refocuses the button; outside click or a link closes; after a sign-in from its "Přihlásit se" the focus moves to the `nav` itself (`tabindex="-1"`, no outline), so no control stays highlighted and the next Tab starts at the logo). `site-footer/`: columns and bottom bar with the privacy link.

## content

- `home/` composes `sections/`: `hero` (full-bleed photo, copy, CTA), `stats-bar` (years counted from the registry entry of 28 October 2005 on the visitor's day), `about`, `projects` and `news`: `Home` asks `GET /api/content/home` once (`features/content/home-api.ts`, `HomeApi`) and passes `items` to both sections; with published items each card shows the photo (`/api/content/media/{id}?w=480` with a 960 px `srcset`, none without a photo), the tag, the date (news, `formatDonationDate`) or the year (projects), the title as a link stretched over the whole card (`.news-link`, `.card-link`) to `/aktuality/<slug>` or `/projekty/<slug>` and the perex; while the list is empty or the request fails, the placeholder cards stay (nothing to click), until the real content arrives (plan v2 task 13), `donate-cta`: preset or custom amount (whole crowns with spaces, hint after leaving the field), QR from `PaymentQrApi` drawn by `app-qr-code` at 200 px (answers are cached per amount and symbol, after the first QR the other preset amounts are fetched ahead so a preset switch asks nothing, only a typed amount is debounced, the latest request wins; while a new QR loads the previous one stays, `aria-busy`, and then morphs into it), "Uložit QR kód" saves a 720 px PNG made in the browser (`qr-platba-nova-nadeje.png`; "Připravujeme obrázek…" while it runs); up to 480 px the QR sits above its text, bank details (account, amount, the signed-in donor's variable symbol, IBAN), each with a copy button; a note says the gift shows in the overview once booked or, without a symbol, to sign in first ("Založit účet", "Přihlásit se" open the dialog; after a sign-in from here the page stays and the focus moves to the "Bankovní spojení" label, now with the donor's symbol); on phones the bank details precede the QR (CSS `order`).
- `detail/` (`content-detail`, both `/aktuality/:slug` and `/projekty/:slug`): loads the article or project by its slug (`NewsApi.publishedBySlug`, `ProjectsApi.publishedBySlug`); "Načítáme stránku…" (`role="status"`), a failure as `role="alert"` "Stránku se nepodařilo načíst." with "Zkusit znovu", a 404 (`content/news-not-found`, `content/project-not-found`) renders `not-found` at the same address; when loaded, the dark intro with "← Všechny aktuality" / "← Všechny projekty" (`/#aktuality`, `/#projekty`), the eyebrow "<tag> · <date>" or "<tag> · <year>", the focused `h1`, the perex as lead, then the photo (`?w=960`, `srcset` 480/960/1440, `alt=""`) and the body split into paragraphs at empty lines (single line breaks kept by `white-space: pre-line`), and "Přispět" (`/#prispet`) with "Zpět na aktuality" / "Zpět na projekty". Texts under `content.detail.*`; spec `content-detail.spec.ts` (`paragraphs()` included).
- `privacy-policy/` ("Zásady ochrany osobních údajů"), `not-found/` ("Tuhle stránku jsme nenašli", links home and to the contact form, `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` while shown because nginx answers 200 for every path).

`contact-api.ts` (`POST /api/contact/messages`); `contact-form/` with honeypot, 429 and generic errors, and a line under the button that the data serves only to handle the message, linking the policy (legitimate interest, no consent wording).

## donations (services and helpers)

- `payment-qr-api.ts`: `paymentQr(amount, variableSymbol, purpose = 'DONATION')` → SPAYD, `qrCode` (module matrix), account; sends `purpose` only for `ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION`.
- `donations-api.ts`: `GET /api/donations/me` and `/me/summary` (the API's `year` filter has no caller); `requestBankCheck()` (POST) and `bankCheck()` (GET) on `/me/bank-check`; `checkBankUntilDone()` posts and polls every 2 s while `checking`, at most 45 times, emitting every answer; `singleReceipt(id)` and `yearlyReceipt(year)` fetch the PDFs as blobs and return `DownloadedFile` (`blob`, `fileName` from `Content-Disposition`: `filename*=UTF-8''` first, then `filename=`, else `potvrzeni-o-daru.pdf` / `potvrzeni-o-darech-<year>.pdf`).
- `bank-accounts-api.ts`: `GET /api/donations/me/bank-accounts` (`ACTIVE`, `AWAITING_PAYMENT`, `DISCONNECTED`, `shared`, `pairingExcluded`, `verificationPaymentOn`, `symbolOffer` with `variableSymbol`, `paymentCount`, `firstMonth`/`lastMonth` as `YYYY-MM`, and `confirmedSymbols`), `POST …/{id}/disconnect|reconnect` (204), `confirmSymbol` / `declineSymbol` on `POST …/{id}/symbol-claim/confirm` (`{ pairedCount, changedYearlyReceipts }`) and `…/decline` (204) with `{ variableSymbol }`; `DonationItem.pairedBy` (`VARIABLE_SYMBOL` | `SENDER_ACCOUNT` | `CONFIRMED_SYMBOL` | null), `senderAccount`, `confirmedSymbol` and `receiptAvailable`.
- `donation-month.ts`: `formatMonth('2025-01')` → `leden 2025` (names `@@donor.month.january` … `december`), `formatMonthRange(first, last)` → `říjen 2026`, `březen–říjen 2026` or `leden 2025 – říjen 2026`, with no-break spaces and no locale data.
- `file-download.ts` `saveBlob(file)`: object URL on a hidden `<a download>` clicked inside the page, revoked in `setTimeout(…, 0)` (Safari and Firefox cancel a synchronously revoked download). `donation-date.ts`: `YYYY-MM-DD` → `15. 03. 2026` without locale data; `formatBankTime` → Prague `18. 10. 2026 10:05`. `donation-amount.ts`: whole crowns without decimals, hellers always two (`1 000,50`). `testing/` fixtures.

## Theme, fonts, assets

- `src/styles/themes/_nova-nadeje.scss` declares the `@font-face` rules and every token on `:root` (inventory in `frontend.md`). Fonts: Playfair Display and Inter as self-hosted variable WOFF2 (latin and latin-ext from Fontsource, OFL licences beside them in `src/styles/themes/fonts/`); the build hashes them into `/media/`, which nginx caches as immutable. `npm run lint:styles` fails on a raw colour, `font-family`, `border-radius` or shadow outside the theme.
- `public/images/`: hero in 1024/1536 px with `srcset` (the source photo is 1536 px wide, so wider screens scale it up), `sizes="max(100vw, 150vh, 900px)"` and `fetchpriority="high"`; about and donate in 720/1440 px; project and news cards at 600 px; every photo below the hero has `loading="lazy"` written before `src`/`srcset` (Angular sets attributes in template order) plus `width`/`height`, so photos hidden on phones are never downloaded there. Convert a new photo with Pillow or `cwebp` at quality about 78; no file is kept that no template names.
- Icons: `favicon.svg` is the logo's star in a square viewBox 840 units wide, strokes 64 (face 52), white fill; its `<style>` hides the face (`g.face`) at 24 px or less (Chromium measures CSS px, so a 16 px image has no face on a 2x screen either; `e2e/home-assets.spec.ts`). `favicon.ico` holds 16, 32 and 48 px renders; `apple-touch-icon.png` is 180 px, viewBox 1060 wide (wider margin for iOS rounding), stroke 40 (face 32) on opaque white. The cloud ImageMagick has no SVG renderer: render with Playwright's Chromium (`omitBackground`) and pack with `convert 16.png 32.png 48.png favicon.ico`.
