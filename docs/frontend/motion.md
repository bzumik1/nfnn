# Motion

- Motion (Jakub 2026-11-16, "Střídmější, méně nápadné jako iOS, ale promyšlené a dokonalé … vše je animované"; agreed by both polish teams, mobile and web, in phase 0): every change of state moves, briefly, with iOS curves, never for its own sake and never holding anything up.
  - Rules: motion explains a change of state; the result never waits for the motion (focus, `close`, `aria-expanded`, a `role="status"` text and the navigation happen at its start, and what leaves is `inert` from its first millisecond), with one named exception: a modal `<dialog>` that leaves, since without `overlay` and `allow-discrete` (which Safari lacks) a closed dialog leaves the top layer at once, so it stays open through its leaving transitions with its panel `inert` and the focus on the dialog itself from the first millisecond, and `close()`, `dismissed` and the return of the focus to the opener come at their end, at the latest their duration plus 0.3 s (R38 of #1331, decision (a), both teams 12/12); what can turn back half way (hover, press, dialog, disclosure, switch) is a `transition` or `@starting-style`, never keyframes; an interaction lasts at most `--duration-base`, a move across the screen at most `--duration-page` (a sheet `--duration-sheet`); longer only the hero, a section moving in, a count-up and a spring settling. Only `transform`, `translate`, `scale`, `rotate`, `opacity` and colours move (plus the press's `filter` and the `box-shadow` of small controls); never `all`, a size (except the one grid row through which Rozbalení and a leaving row open and close, D9), a position, `background` (only `background-color`), the radius of `backdrop-filter`, `outline` or a focus shadow, and no lasting `will-change`; the focus ring never animates. What leaves without a finger takes the next shorter duration; opacity is full at about 60 % of a move; text never reflows during a change (a swapped label reserves the width of its longer wording, a screen reader hears only the final value, once). A busy button is locked at once (`disabled` or `aria-disabled`) and carries `aria-busy="true"` until the work ends; its visible label ("Ukládáme…") changes only after `--delay-loading` and turns back at once when the work ends, so an answer faster than the delay never changes the label, and a new start waits the whole delay again (#1250, replacing D1 variant a′). The accessible name stays the resting label while the work runs, the busy label being `aria-hidden` (explicit exception from D1/#1250, Label in Name, R66: a locked button cannot be operated by voice, and VoiceOver and NVDA do not read a renamed focused button again); after the work the name equals the visible label again. The start is said instead by the card's existing `role="status"` line, after the same delay and only if the work still runs, with the busy label's own text (same i18n id, no new sentence), visually hidden so the card does not move, and the result replaces it; both come from one signal (`showsBusy` of `shared/ui/busy-label`, read through the card's `BusyAnnouncement`), so the line and the label change in the same frame, with no second timer. `shared/ui/busy-label` keeps both labels in one grid cell, so the button holds the width of the longer one where its row has room (where it has none, the unseen label gives way and the button stays within its row) and the label is never shortened, centres them in it, lets them wrap between words within the button at enlarged text, fades the coming label in by a `transition` (`motion.state(opacity)`; `--duration-reduced` under reduced motion) while the leaving one is gone at once (no transition, so no frame shows it broken inside a word, R65), and announces nothing itself: no `aria-live` on the button, no second live region and no focus move. Where no `BusyAnnouncement` is provided (no status line says the start: the administration until #1380, the contact form, the sign-in, `/pripojit`), the label falls back to naming the button by what it shows (R69): after `--delay-loading` the busy label is the accessible name, the resting one `aria-hidden`, and the button gets no `aria-busy`, so a screen reader still hears the start.
  - Time from the tap to the motion (D1, mobile team 6/6, 2026-11-21; the same for an opening, ‹ and a swipe below `nav`): from the tap (or the finger's first move) until both still copies of the step are placed at most 150 ms when the page's code is already loaded, and at most 300 ms for a first opening that loads its chunks, the row holding its pressed state until then (not built yet: #1949); the page then moves at the latest in the second frame after the busy frame that follows the placing, and no frame in the first half of the motion travels more than 1.5 times what one frame at the device's steady pace travels at that point of the spring (rule R1, replacing rule R's „the first moved frame at most a fifth of the way“, which a steady 30 frames a second runner failed and a single long frame later in the motion passed), nor ever back. Nothing moves a step's start once the step is let go (#1538 round 16): the page un-pauses the held motions (`play()`) and the browser sets each start time as it commits the frame that first draws it, so that frame shows the motion at its start; a start the page set itself was a frame time already past, and the first visible frame was 67–83 ms into the motion. The re-timing of rounds 7 to 15 moved the start later when a frame came late, but the main thread cannot know what the compositor has already drawn: on the Prodesk (CDP 3×) the compositor started Správa's scrim from the first start and each later move showed as a step back, the ground going 0 → 0.87 → 0.45 → 0.68 → 0.87 in screencast frames 17–33 ms apart (`top-area.spec.ts`, the step from the account sheet into Správa, 4 of 10 runs at 2 workers); a motion has no start time while it is pending, so the re-timing loop could no longer move anything and is gone, and the app only publishes the steady frame (`publishSteadyFrame`). A main-thread stall in the first half therefore shows in the probe's frames as one long frame while the compositor runs on. The clock of the motion starts only once the copies are placed and drawn (`--held` until then, `PageTransitions.startMoving`). `e2e/back-matrix.spec.ts` and `e2e/sprava-back-matrix.spec.ts` (the walk of every section and subpage of Správa, apart so that the CI shards split the matrix) check it in Chromium on every row of the matrix.
