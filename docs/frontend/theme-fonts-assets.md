# Theme, fonts, assets

- The theme is a folder chosen at build time: `src/themes/nova-nadeje/_theme.scss` declares the `@font-face` rules and every token on `:root` (inventory in "Frontend conventions in detail", UI, below), `styles.scss` loads it as `@use 'theme'` through `stylePreprocessorOptions.includePaths` (`src/styles` and the theme folder), and the configuration `control-theme` swaps in `src/themes/control/` (a deliberately different look with system fonts, built and checked by `e2e/theme.spec.ts` in CI only; `scripts/check-themes.mjs`, part of `npm run lint:styles`, keeps the token sets equal). The files of a theme's look live in its `assets/` folder and are served at the site root (each build configuration maps its theme's `assets/` with `output: "/"`, plan v3 2.22): `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `app-icon.svg` and `images/logo.svg` and `images/logo-on-dark.svg`, at the addresses they had in `public/`; the control theme carries copies of the same files for now. `check-themes.mjs` fails when a theme lacks a file of `nova-nadeje` or has one more, when a theme file is also in `public/` (the build would serve one of them unseen), and when a build serves no `assets/` of its own theme or another theme's. The receipt PDF still draws the logo from `receipt-logo.ts` (`receipt-logo.spec.ts` compares it with the `nova-nadeje` logo), so a theme with another logo also changes that file. The theme also carries the tokens of the app layout (2.22, used from 2.23): the floating tab bar of decision 2.1 in the one chrome material of the design plan (slice 1, "Glass and no glow", Jakub 2026-11-06, plan sections 2.2 and 2.6; - Light and dark appearance (#347; decided 2026-11-06 under Jakub's instruction of that day that the app behave like a native iOS app): the site follows the device's Light / Dark setting. `index.html` declares `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">`, so the browser paints its canvas dark before the styles load, and each theme ends with a block `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { :root { color-scheme: dark; … } }` that only overrides colour tokens (and `--filter-ink`, `--blend-ink`), so the light look is the top-level `:root` unchanged and native controls (date fields, selects, checkboxes, scroll bars) follow through `color-scheme`. `nova-nadeje` dark, as an iOS designer and an accessibility expert agreed: the page and the cells `#121826` (a navy-tinted near-black, not pure black: less halation for astigmatic readers and the brand's navy stays visible; `#10141f` until the design slice s3), `--color-surface-alt` `#080b12` for quiet grounds inside them, the navy bands `#13213f` and `--color-surface-inverse-strong` `#05070c` (the former footer's), text `#e6e8ee` (not white, less glare) to soft `#9aa3b2`, links and focus ring `#6ea8ff`, softened danger, warning and success colours, a darker backdrop, and its own block for `prefers-contrast: more` in the dark (placed after the light one, which would otherwise win). A colour used both as text and as a fill splits in two: `--color-accent` (text, links, borders) becomes the light blue, while `--color-accent-fill` (the filled buttons, the selected amount, the stats band, badges, and the glow shadows) stays `#2563eb` with white text (5.17:1), since no single blue reads both on the dark page and under white text; `--color-danger-fill` likewise for danger buttons, and `--color-surface-quote` for the quote bubble that used `--color-text` as its ground. Each new token equals the old value in the light look. What must stay light stays light: the QR tokens (dark modules on white, so banks scan them) and the logo tokens that paint its star, every `--receipt-*` token (the PDF never depends on the device), the manifest colours (checked against the top-level `:root` only) and the receipt-signature previews of `/sprava` on `--color-paper` and `--color-paper-muted`, which show the ink as it prints. - Appearance choice (#637; Jakub 2026-11-06, the three choices 2026-11-12, the look 2026-11-13): the account sheet's head carries one round button beside the close button that cycles Automaticky (monitor) → Světlý (sun) → Tmavý (moon), named `Vzhled: <choice>`; on a wide screen it is plain like the dialog's ×, beside it. `core/appearance/appearance.ts` (`Appearance`, started by `appearanceAtStart`) keeps the choice only on the device, in `localStorage` under `nfnn.appearance` (removed for Automaticky; every read and write in try/catch, so a refused storage keeps the choice for the visit), and applies it as `data-appearance="light"|"dark"` on `<html>` (none for Automaticky) and as the content of `<meta name="color-scheme">` (`light`, `dark`, or `light dark`). `public/appearance.js`, a classic script in the `<head>` of `index.html` beside `boot-guard.js` (the CSP refuses an inline one; the service worker keeps it with the shell), applies the kept choice before the body is parsed, so neither the launch screen nor the page paints the other appearance first, even while the app's code is still on its way (`scripts/appearance-script.test.mjs`, and an e2e that holds `main-*.js` back). Signing out (`AuthStore.forget` while a donor was signed in: the sign-out button, another tab's sign-out, a refused session) sets Automaticky again, since only a signed-in donor reaches the switch and the next visitor of the device could not undo it (Jakub 2026-11-14). The Smart Invert block `@media (inverted-colors: inverted)` names `:root:not([data-appearance='light'])`, so it still outranks the dark blocks, and `check-themes.mjs` fails a theme whose inverted-colors values lose in any appearance. A `storage` event carries a choice made in another tab of the site to this one without a reload, and `Appearance.dark()` (the choice, or the device under Automaticky) drives the logos. Each theme writes its dark tokens once, in the mixins `dark-appearance` and `dark-appearance-more-contrast`, and includes them under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { :root:not([data-appearance='light']) }` and under `:root[data-appearance='dark']` (the more-contrast one under the matching `prefers-contrast: more` queries), while `:root[data-appearance='light']` only sets `color-scheme: light`; `check-themes.mjs` expands the mixins and checks the forced dark appearance on a light device and the forced light one on a dark device too, and `make-startup-images.mjs` reads the dark surface from the device block. Switching needs no server (`WORKS_WITHOUT_CONNECTION`). `e2e/dark-appearance.spec.ts` switches from the account sheet on a light and a dark device, checks the page, the logo and `color-scheme`, the choice after a reload, another open tab following and the return to Automaticky, and runs axe in the forced dark and the forced light.
